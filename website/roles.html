Everyone knows a little. Nobody knows it all.

Role cards are published at game start, abilities live on a personal dashboard, and the Overseer tracks every effect, relationship and whisper behind the scenes.

Abilities The intel network

Your role, on a dashboard

🧾 Role packages

Hosts register pinned-message role packages and role cards/teams, then run the publishing pipeline — .publishroles → .reviewroles → .approvepublish — so every player gets an official sheet. The library keeps the codex with stats, winrates and relations.

📋 Player dashboard

.dashboard renders each player's active and passive abilities, visit blocking (.vb), action log and house locks. Presets (.preset, .ospreset) let hosts roll out ability packages by category.

The deep version: relationships and habits, built up across the whole game.

A passive who-met-whom adjacency matrix — the host can replay the entire social graph.

BOARD

.setboard

The Overseer Intelligence Board: per-player effect cards in a dedicated SQLite DB, refreshed with .refreshcards.

LOG

Action log channel

Central logs intercept role/bot mentions and track player actions, with approval buttons for hosts.

Meetings & whispers

🤝 Meeting rooms

The meeting cog runs the full scheduler — requests, cooldowns, blocked users and archives (.meeting, .endmeeting, .meetingconfig), wired into the meetup matrix so every private chat feeds the social graph.

👁️ Message tracking

.statss, .serverstats and .start_tracking expose message-count metrics — who talks, when, and how much. Great for hosts judging activity and for meta-gamers counting tells.
