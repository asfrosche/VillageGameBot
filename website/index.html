The village is a living map.

The Village Game is a 30–40 player social deduction bot. Your Discord server becomes the village: channels are rooms, players physically move, knock on doors, whisper and scheme — while Overseers run the whole world from one panel.

What makes it a game

MOVEMENT

🚪Channels are rooms

.move, .knock and .home let players genuinely walk the map. Hosts can force-move, lock houses and auto-route traffic. No more fake "rooms" — space matters.

PHASES

☀️Day & night cycles

A rigid Day/Night structure driven by the host. Votes and lynching by day; sneaking, killing and role actions under cover of darkness.

META

🪙Everything is host-tunable

Economy balances, role packages, status effects, cooldowns and calendars are configured per server and published to players through the built-in help hub.

TOOLS

🕵️Overseer toolkit

Follow, track and stalk relationships, an Intelligence Board, status manager, action logs, transcripts and one-click resets keep hosts in total control.

ENGINES

⚙️Sub-systems built-in

Blood on the Clocktower, a World Cup draft/simulator, a fast analytics pipeline and a web dashboard ship with the same bot.

DATA

📊Every action recorded

Every command execution is captured to SQLite and exposed through .analytics, .failures and a FastAPI dashboard.

The world at a glance

🎭 One bot, three theatres

  • Village Game — the main living-map mafia experience.
  • Blood on the Clocktower — full BOTC engine with roles, jinxes, fabled and scripts.
  • World Cup simulator — community drafts and V1–V5 tactical match simulations.

🧩 Architecture

  • Cog-based — ~40 isolated cogs registered in main.py, some loaded lazily on ready.
  • SQLite-first — settings, invites, deadlist, players and per-feature DBs, with legacy JSON auto-migration.
  • Analytics pipeline — instrumentation hooks before_invoke/after_invoke to capture every command.
