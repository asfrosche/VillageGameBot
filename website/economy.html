The economy at a glance

WALLET

.collect

RC balances and wallets are per-server. Players collect income and spend it in the shop.

SHOP

.buy / sell

Items have real prices. .inventory tracks what you carry, .trade swaps between players.

DROPS

.drop

Interactive item drops run on persistent buttons with SQLite-backed atomic claims and an expiration loop — one click, one claim, no double-grabs.

LADDER

.leaderboard

Net worth rankings — bragging rights and a juicy target for thief roles.

The quiet machinery

📌 Sticky messages

Silent, cooldown-based re-pinning per channel (.sticky set, .sticky cooldown) — reposts only on new chat, never pings.

🔔 Bump reminders

Watch one or more channels for Disboard /bump and get a nudge after a configurable delay (.sticky bumpchannel, bumplist, bumphours).

📅 Calendars & meetups

EN .calendar and IT .calendario wizards schedule games in their own SQLite DB; meetings and birthdays are announced on loop.

🕰️ Timezones & locations

.mysetloc, .localtime, .near, .mapp — real-world location mapping via timezonefinder & geopy.

📊 Analytics

Every command is instrumented (before/after_invoke). Query via .analytics, .failures, export with .analyticsexport, or open the FastAPI dashboard.

📚 Game library

lib / libit: a shared codex of games, roles, stats, winrates and relations across EN & IT.
