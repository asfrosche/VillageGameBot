The Round

From empty server to full village.

A game is built in four moves: map the world, place the roles, open the doors, then let the Day/Night loop take over.

The game loop Setup

1 · Building the village

Map categories, roles, channels

The host runs .setup <num_houses> to bind the world — Alive, Dead, Sponsor, Alt and log channels — to the datastore. .roleset, .channelset and .categoryset fine-tune the layout.

Spawn players at home

.home spawns a player, .owner assigns ownership. Presets and role packages register role cards so everyone gets a clean ability sheet.

Open the doors

The host flips phases with .day / .night, sweeps channels with .broom, and the game is live.

Navigation — you live in this map

MOVE

.move

Walk to another room. Rendering happens live — join/leave messages reflect who is physically where.

KNOCK

.knock

Request entry to another player's house. The owner approves or ignores; hosts can force or cancel pending knocks.

SNEAK

.renmove

Stealth movement. .renmove quietly relocates while suppressing join/leave messages — perfect for killers with a hall pass.

Every player can find the whole map instantly — .map and .where answer "where is the action?"

2 · The Day/Night loop

Day

Talk, accuse, vote

Players gather, share intel, level accusations and vote. Hosts manipulate votes, skip night and read .votelist at a glance.

Vote / lynch

.vote

Classic lynching with .abstain and .removevote. A token-based trial track (.accuse, .voten) runs alongside from the Nominations cog.

Night

Kill & scheme

Under .night, sneaking happens, role actions resolve, and the host logs kills with .dead / .deadrole.

Reset

.end

When the world shifts, hosts rebuild: .destroy, .rebuild, .newpc, .mergepc, and .endgame to close the chapter cleanly.

3 · Living world, live host

🌍 World shaping

  • .decay / .destroy — rooms collapse and are rebuilt over time.
  • .close / .public / .private — flip room access live.
  • .setowner, .movehouse, .rename — properties change hands mid-game.

🎛️ Host control room

  • .settings — inspect everything bound to the world.
  • .log — full transcripts for review.
  • .timer, .roll, .narrate — pacing tools for the table.
  • .skipnight, .resetvotes — keep the tempo.
